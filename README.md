FridgeLight: the worst app idea ever pitched — a smart-home app that charges $5 a month to send you a reassuring live alert about your fridge light every time you open the door to check whether it stays on with the door closed.

The pitch works because the app can only ever alert you at the exact moment the light is on anyway: it solves a problem nobody has, with total deadpan confidence, and can never once answer the question it exists to answer.
